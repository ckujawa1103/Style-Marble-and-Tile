How a job runs

  1. Walk the job

    We come look at the space, take rough measurements, and talk through material and layout.

  2. Written estimate

    Material, square footage, and labor broken out separately. Every job is different and the season affects both what it costs and when we can get to it, so we work the time and the price out with you here rather than quoting a number before we have seen it.

  3. Permits, if your township needs them

    We are licensed, insured, and permitted across the local municipalities, so filings get handled before anyone shows up rather than halfway through.

  4. Template

    Once cabinets or substrate are set, we template to the actual space — not to the plans.

  5. Fabricate

    Slab layout for veining and seams, cutouts, and edge profiles in the shop.

  6. Install

    Set, seamed, sealed, and cleaned up. We walk it with you before we leave.
